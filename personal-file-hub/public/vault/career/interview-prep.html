Interview Prep

Comprehensive checklist for senior engineering interviews

📋 Before the Interview
Research
Research the company's data stack and engineering blog
Look for blog posts, job descriptions, LinkedIn engineers
Understand their product and how data flows through it
Review your last 3 projects for STAR stories
Situation · Task · Action · Result format
Prepare 5 insightful questions to ask the panel
Test video setup, lighting, and microphone
🧠 Technical Prep
Practice
System design: design a real-time streaming pipeline
Kafka → Spark → Snowflake architecture walkthrough
SQL: window functions, CTEs, query optimization
Python: data processing patterns, async, testing
Review data modeling: star schema vs Kimball
Prepare to whiteboard: idempotency, at-least-once delivery
💬 Behavioral Questions
Critical
"Tell me about a time you fixed a critical production issue"
"Describe your most impactful data engineering project"
"How do you handle disagreements with stakeholders?"
"What's a technical decision you'd make differently today?"
